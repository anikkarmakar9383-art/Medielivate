Quick First Aid Guides

Heart Attack

Call emergency services immediately and keep patient calm.

Burn Injury

Cool the burn under running water for 10 minutes.

Bleeding

Apply firm pressure with clean cloth to stop bleeding.

Breathing Problem

Help patient sit upright and loosen tight clothing.

Stroke

Look for facial drooping and call emergency help quickly.

Choking

Perform Heimlich maneuver if person cannot breathe.
